The walk

The original World Coin (WDC) is a real, independently verifiable project — not something made up for this revival. Here's its actual path, scroll to follow it.

May 2013

World Coin launches as a Scrypt-based chain, built in the same lineage as Litecoin, with block times fast enough for everyday spending rather than pure speculation.

December 2013

WDC hits its all-time high, trading close to $0.90 during that year's altcoin run. That peak is real and independently recorded, not a claim invented for this revival.

2014 – 2020

Volume thins out, but the chain doesn't disappear. At least one real network upgrade lands in this window a move to AuxPoW merged mining with an updated difficulty algorithm — kept alive by a small remaining community.

2023

Sam Altman's identity-verification project launches under the strikingly similar name Worldcoin, ticker WLD. It's unrelated to the 2013 coin, but its funding and reach pull nearly all public attention to the new name.

2023 – 2025

The original WDC chain goes largely dormant in the shadow of WLD's launch. Still technically alive on-chain, but trading activity drops close to zero.

2026

$WDC is revived as a new token on Solana via pump.fun, carrying the original name and story forward into a new chain and a new community.

Now

Small, real, and rebuilding in public — arcade shipped, more coming. This page updates as things change. That's the whole point of it.
